Student questions

The "wait, how does this work?" list.

Does Slima write the book for me?
No. Slima organizes, remembers, and critiques — but the words stay yours. It never auto-generates pages of prose into your manuscript, so the work you submit is your own.
Do you own or train on my writing?
No on both. You own 100% of your work. Your manuscript is encrypted, scoped to your account, and never used to train AI models. Your teacher can't see your drafts either — unless you choose to share them.
What does the free plan actually include?
Both Writing Studio and Script Studio with full editing, version control, cloud sync, offline mode, and export to .docx, .epub, and Markdown. The paid plans add the AI coach and reading reports — but the studio itself is free forever.
How do I get the 20% student discount?
When you sign up, enter your teacher's name and email in the educator field. We verify against their educator profile and apply 20% off Starter or Pro automatically — no promo code needed. The discount stays active as long as your teacher remains a Slima Educator.
Can I bring in a draft I've already started?
Yes. Drag in a .docx, .md, or plain text file. Slima reads the chapter structure and indexes the whole project, so full-book memory works from day one.
What if I cancel? Do I lose my work?
Never. Cancel any time and you keep full editing, sync, and export on the free tier. Export every chapter — with its version history — to .docx or .md whenever you want. Your work travels with you.
